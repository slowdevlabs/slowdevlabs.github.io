Website Privacy

SlowDevLabs website privacy notice

This page explains the minimal privacy practices used on the SlowDevLabs landing site itself. It does not replace the privacy policies for TimeTape, 돌리GO, or CUE.

Last updated: May 21, 2026 · Contact: slowdevlabs@gmail.com

Overview

This site is used as an app introduction hub.

The website is operated to introduce SlowDevLabs apps and connect them to their store pages. App data and app-level policies are managed separately on each app's own pages.

Analytics

Basic web analytics tools may be used.

This website may use basic analytics tools such as Google Analytics to understand website traffic and improve the experience. Website analytics apply only at the site level and do not affect app policies.

Data Collection

No accounts, comments, or direct submissions.

  • The website does not include a sign-in or account system.
  • The website does not offer comments or user posts.
  • The website does not provide direct data submission flows.
  • App data is handled by each app's privacy policy and data flow.

Cookies

Small cookies may be used for analytics.

The site may use standard cookies or similar technologies for basic analytics and traffic measurement. You can manage or clear cookies in your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

External services are limited to analytics and app links.

The site may reference Google Analytics and link out to App Store and app-specific privacy pages. Those third-party services have their own policies.

App Privacy Policies

Each app keeps its own privacy policy.
