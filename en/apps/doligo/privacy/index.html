Doligo Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026

Doligo (the “App”) takes your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes and how your information is protected.

Doligo currently works without sign-up, and your travel results and roulette records are stored only on your device.

1. Information we collect

The App does not currently collect personal information that can directly identify you, such as your name, email address, phone number, or account information.

The following information created inside the App may be stored locally on your device:

  • Destination roulette results
  • Food roulette results
  • Today's Doligo progress status
  • Roulette records created while using the App

This information is stored on your device only to support app functionality and is not sent to the developer's server.

2. Remote data usage

To keep roulette candidate data such as cities and foods up to date, the App may load remote JSON data. During this process, the App may download candidate data files, but it does not send your roulette results or personal records to the server.

3. Map app links

When you use features such as “I'm going for real” or “I'm going to eat,” the App may open external map apps such as Naver Map, Kakao Map, T Map, Google Maps, or Apple Maps.

When you use an external map app, that service's privacy policy applies. Doligo does not collect or store location information or search history processed by external map apps.

4. Sharing features

The App may provide a feature that lets you share your selected roulette results as an image or text. Sharing happens only when you choose to do so, and the App does not automatically send results outside the device.

5. Contacting us

If you send us an email through the App's “Contact us” feature, the email content you write and the sender email address may be used to handle your inquiry. We use inquiry details only for replies and issue resolution.

6. Third-party sharing and ads

The App does not currently sell or share your personal information with third parties. It also does not currently use any separate advertising SDK or analytics SDK.

If advertising, analytics tools, or paid features are added in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and notify you in the app or in the App Store listing if needed.

7. Data retention and deletion

Roulette results and progress status are stored on your device. If you delete the App, the app data stored on the device may also be deleted.

If the App provides a record deletion feature, you can use it to delete your local records.

8. Children's privacy

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children. It is provided without requiring sign-up or personal identification information.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated due to changes in the App's features, laws, or operating policies. When changes are made, the effective date on this page will be updated.

10. Contact
