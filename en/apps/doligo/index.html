DOLIGO

Let the
wheel decide.

A light, spontaneous roulette app for travel plans and food choices.
Doligo is not a search app for picking destinations. It is a travel roulette app that leaves today's choices to the wheel.
View Features

Doligo is currently available in Korean only.

FEATURES

Light, fast,
and ready for today.

Pick a destination or a meal without overthinking it. Doligo keeps the choice quick and playful.
🎲
Fate mode
Roll through regions and travel directions step by step until the destination is decided.
⚡️
Fast mode
Choose transport and theme, then spin the city wheel right away.
🍜
Food roulette
Decide what to eat after arrival with another quick spin.
🗺️
Map app links
Connects to Naver Map, Kakao Map, T Map, Google Maps, and Apple Maps.
No personal data is collected.
Doligo works without sign-up, and roulette results plus progress are stored only on your device.
