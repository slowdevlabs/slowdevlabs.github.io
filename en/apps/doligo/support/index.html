Doligo Support

Support and contact page for the Doligo app

Doligo is a random roulette app that makes travel and food choices playful and easy.

Doligo currently works without sign-up, and your travel results and roulette records are stored only on your device.

  • Feature suggestions and improvement ideas
  • Other app-related inquiries
