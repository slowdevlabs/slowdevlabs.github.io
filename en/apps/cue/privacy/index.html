Privacy Policy

CUE: KPOP Memory Challenge is a quiz app that loads preview URLs from the Apple Music catalog at runtime and plays them for quiz purposes. We keep the policy simple and transparent.

Last updated: 2026-08-24

What CUE does

CUE is a KPOP memory quiz app. It uses short Apple Music preview clips during gameplay so players can identify songs and artists. The app does not store or redistribute full music files.

  • CUE uses Apple Music preview only for gameplay based on song identification, not as background music or for independent listening.
  • Full-track listening continues in the Apple Music service.
  • Music metadata, previews, artwork, and trademarks belong to their respective rights holders.

Music data

Preview clips are used only while the app is running. CUE does not save, resell, or share full music files.

Advertising

CUE uses Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded advertisements. Advertising services may collect device identifiers, advertising data, and usage information in accordance with Google's policies. CUE does not require a direct user account sign-in.

Analytics

CUE uses Firebase Analytics to understand how features are used and to improve the overall user experience. Analytics data may include gameplay events, feature usage, and app interaction information. This information is used in aggregate form and is not intended to personally identify users.

Crash Reporting

CUE uses Firebase Crashlytics to monitor app stability and diagnose technical issues. Crash reports may include device model, operating system version, app version, and technical diagnostic information. This information is used solely to improve reliability, stability, and performance.

Feedback and support requests

When you choose to use the Send Feedback feature, CUE may process the feedback you enter and any optional contact information you provide. Basic app and device context, such as the app version, operating system version, language, and platform, may also be submitted to help diagnose issues and improve the service. If you choose to attach an image, that image may also be submitted with your feedback. This information is sent only when you explicitly submit feedback and is used for support and product improvement.

Local storage

App settings, gameplay data, cached quiz data, and other locally stored preferences may be stored on the device so the app can function properly and provide a smoother experience.

Ad settings

Device-level ad settings can be managed through Apple or Google platform settings.

Your choices

  • Using Send Feedback is optional. You can continue using the app's main features without submitting feedback.
  • Providing an email address is optional.
  • You can contact us through the Support page if you have a privacy question.
  • You can manage device-level permissions and ad settings through Apple and Google platform settings.
  • You can clear local app data from your device if you want to reset cached content or preferences.

For privacy questions, use the support page.
