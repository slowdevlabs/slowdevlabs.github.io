Privacy

SymbolicDrop privacy policy

SymbolicDrop is designed to stay local. This policy covers the app and its support pages, and keeps the web presence intentionally small.

No account system

SymbolicDrop does not require an account, login, or user profile.

No analytics

The app does not collect analytics, usage tracking, or behavioral telemetry.

No cloud sync

SymbolicDrop does not sync your files or settings to a cloud service.

No file uploads

The app does not upload your files to a remote server.

Local behavior

File operations stay on your device.

SymbolicDrop works with local filesystem paths and local Finder destinations only. It does not create a network-backed workflow or send file contents elsewhere.

Tracking

No user tracking is built in.

SymbolicDrop does not include user tracking, fingerprinting, or advertising tools in the app.
