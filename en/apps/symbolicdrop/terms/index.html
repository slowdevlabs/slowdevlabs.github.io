Symbolic link utility disclaimer

SymbolicDrop is a utility for creating symbolic links on macOS. Symbolic links point to existing files or folders. If the original item is moved, renamed, deleted, or becomes unavailable, the symbolic link may stop working.

You are responsible for managing your own files, folders, and symbolic links. SymbolicDrop does not back up, sync, recover, or repair files.
