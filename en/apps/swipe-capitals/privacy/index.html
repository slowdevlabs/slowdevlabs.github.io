Privacy

Swipe: Capitals privacy policy

Swipe: Capitals is designed to keep quiz play simple and local. This page explains what the app stores on your device and what it does not collect.

Local stats only No account required No cloud sync

Last updated: August 26, 2026

No account system

Swipe: Capitals does not require a sign-up, login, or user profile.

Local stats

Swipe: Capitals may store progress, streaks, and session results on your device so the quiz can resume smoothly.

No analytics

Swipe: Capitals does not currently use analytics tools to track your activity inside the app.

No cloud sync

Your game progress is not synced to a remote account or cloud service.

Support email

If you contact us by email, we use the message and sender address only to review and answer your request.

What the app stores locally

To keep the game moving, Swipe: Capitals may store the following information on your device only:

  • Completed round history
  • Accuracy, streak, and score statistics
  • App preferences and local gameplay settings
  • Progress state needed to resume or review play

This information stays on your device unless you remove the app or clear the local data.

How the app uses data

Swipe: Capitals uses local data to show your progress, make replay feel smoother, and keep your practice history available offline.

  • The app does not send quiz results to SlowDevLabs servers.
  • The app does not require personal information to function.
  • The app may use system features such as haptics or audio feedback, but those features are handled on device.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to respond to your request.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended to collect personal information from children. Because it does not require an account or profile, the app can be used without sharing identity information with us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes, new features are added, or legal requirements change. The page date will be updated when that happens.
