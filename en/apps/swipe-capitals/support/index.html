If you're reporting a bug, include your device model, iOS version, and the capital round you were on.

Common questions

The app is not loading a round

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue continues, send us the steps you took before the problem appeared.

My stats look wrong

Swipe: Capitals stores statistics locally on your device. If something looks off, email us a screenshot or short note.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The app is designed to keep core gameplay and local stats available without an account or network connection.

How do I request a feature?

Send a short note with the idea and the problem it would solve. We read feature requests carefully.

What to include in a bug report

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • The capital question or round state, if you remember it
