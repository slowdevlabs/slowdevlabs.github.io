Send feedback from the app

For bug reports, suggestions, and general feedback, you can use Send Feedback directly from Swipe: Flags Settings.

You can optionally include an email address for a reply and attach an image or screenshot to help explain your feedback.

Contact us

For questions, bug reports, or feature ideas, email us at:

Email: slowdevlabs+swipeflags@gmail.com

If you're contacting us by email about a bug, include your device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem.

The app is not loading a round

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue continues, send us the steps you took before the problem appeared.

My stats look wrong

Swipe: Flags stores statistics locally on your device. If something looks off, email us a screenshot or a short description of the issue.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The app is designed to keep core gameplay and local stats available without an account or network connection.

How do I ask for a feature?

Send us a short message with the idea and what problem it would solve for you. We read feature requests carefully.

What to include in a bug report

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • The steps to reproduce the issue, if you know them
