Privacy

Swipe: Flags privacy policy

Swipe: Flags is designed to keep quiz play simple and local. This page explains what the app stores on your device and what it does not collect.

Local stats only No account required Ad-supported

Last updated: August 10, 2026

No account system

Swipe: Flags does not require a sign-up, login, or user profile.

Advertising

Swipe: Flags uses Google AdMob to show ads in the app. Google may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data according to Google's policies.

No analytics

Swipe: Flags does not currently use analytics tools to track your activity across the app.

No cloud sync

Your game progress is not synced to a remote account or cloud service.

Feedback

When you choose Send Feedback, Swipe: Flags sends your feedback to SlowDevLabs so we can review it and investigate issues.

What the app stores locally

To keep the game moving, Swipe: Flags may store the following information on your device only:

  • Completed round history
  • Accuracy, streak, and score statistics
  • App preferences and local gameplay settings
  • Progress state needed to resume or review play

This information stays on your device unless you remove the app or otherwise clear the local data.

How the app uses data

Swipe: Flags uses local data to show your progress, make replay feel smoother, and keep your practice history available offline.

  • The app does not send quiz results to SlowDevLabs servers.
  • The app does not require personal information to function.
  • The app may use system features such as haptics or audio feedback, but those features are handled on device.
  • Feedback information is sent to SlowDevLabs only when you choose to submit feedback through the app.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback through Swipe: Flags, the information you submit is sent to SlowDevLabs so we can review your feedback, investigate problems, and respond when necessary.

Feedback submissions include your feedback message and limited technical information such as the app version, OS version, and language. You may optionally provide an email address for a reply and attach an image or screenshot to help explain your feedback.

  • Providing an email address or image attachment is optional.
  • Swipe: Flags does not upload an image unless you explicitly select it and submit the feedback.
  • Attached images are retained for 30 days after submission.
  • Other feedback information is retained for 90 days after submission and is automatically deleted when the retention period expires.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended to collect personal information from children. Because it does not require an account or profile, the app can be used without sharing identity information with us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes, new features are added, or legal requirements change. The page date will be updated when that happens.
