A fast flag quiz game where each swipe helps you learn the world a little better.

See gameplay Explore features

Gameplay

Short rounds, quick feedback, easy replay.

Swipe: Flags keeps the experience focused on quick decisions, clean feedback, and practice that is easy to return to.

  • Fast rounds Each session stays short so it is easy to finish a round and start another one.
  • Clean feedback Simple responses and clear progress help players stay focused on the flags.
  • Local progress Stats and round history stay on device so the app can remain lightweight.

Features

Built for fast play and steady memory.

  • Swipe to Answer Move the flag up or down to pick the right country without slowing the round.
  • 195 Countries A broad launch pool gives the game room to teach and surprise.
  • Personal Statistics Track accuracy, streaks, strongest flags, and the ones worth revisiting.
  • Voice Feedback Helpful encouragement in English, 한국어, and Español.
  • Offline Play Core gameplay and flag content are available locally on device.

Languages

English, 한국어, Español.

Swipe: Flags keeps the interface simple while supporting three languages for feedback and play.

Privacy

Local by design.

Swipe: Flags keeps game progress, answer history, and preferences on your device. The app does not require an account and keeps core gameplay available offline. Advertising is provided separately through Google AdMob, and information is sent to SlowDevLabs only when you choose to submit feedback.
