Codex Gauge Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Codex Gauge is a macOS menu bar utility created by SlowDevLabs. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Codex Gauge works locally on your Mac. It does not require an account and does not upload your Codex usage data to SlowDevLabs.

Information we collect

Codex Gauge does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, account details, or API keys.

The app may read local Codex environment information on your Mac to display available usage status. This processing is performed locally for app functionality.

Analytics and tracking

Codex Gauge does not include app analytics, advertising SDKs, or user tracking.

Data uploads

Codex Gauge does not upload your Codex usage data, local environment data, or personal information to SlowDevLabs servers.

Local storage

The app may store preferences or display settings locally on your Mac. If you delete the app and its related local data, those settings may be removed.

Support email

If you contact SlowDevLabs by email, the email address and message content you provide may be used to reply to your request and resolve the issue.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app behavior, legal requirements, or operating policies change. The effective date will be updated when changes are made.
